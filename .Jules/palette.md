## 2024-02-14 - Inline Confirmation Pattern
**Learning:** Destructive actions in constrained UI spaces benefit significantly from a two-step inline confirmation (Click -> 'Confirm?' -> Click) that resets on blur. This avoids the disruption of modal dialogs while preventing accidental deletions.
**Action:** Apply this "inline confirm" pattern to other destructive icon-only buttons in the future to maintain flow while ensuring safety.
